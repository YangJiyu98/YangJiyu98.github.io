# Finn support

**Contact:** yang.jiyu.jy@gmail.com. Say which watch and iPhone you have, and what happened.

## What Finn needs

- An Apple Watch for swimming with your buddy. Depth and water temperature need an Apple Watch with a depth sensor (Apple Watch Ultra, or Series 10 and later); on other watches Finn still times your swim, counts it and brings animals.
- An iPhone for the logbook, aquarium and wallpapers.

## Common questions

**When does a new animal turn up?** After ten minutes in the water, at most once an hour. Where you swim decides who. The odds are the same for everyone, and nothing changes them.

**My swim isn't on my iPhone yet.** Open Finn on the iPhone with your watch nearby. Swims travel straight from the watch to the phone, with no server in between.

**Does Finn work as a dive computer?** No. Finn is made for shallow water and gives no decompression or ascent information. Always swim and dive with proper training and instruments.

**How do I delete a swim?** On iPhone, open the swim and tap Delete swim; on the watch, swipe it in the logbook. It goes from both devices, and animals you have already met stay in your aquarium.

**Where is my data?** Only on your own watch and iPhone, and in Apple Health if you let Finn write there. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
