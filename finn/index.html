Finn: Swim & Snorkel Buddy

FAQSupport

Finn is a free app for Apple Watch and iPhone. A pixel animal swims with you on your wrist while you swim or snorkel, your swims are saved to a logbook, and after ten minutes in the water someone new might turn up and join your aquarium.

Get Finn on the App StoreFree. No ads, no account.

Who it's for

Finn is for people who swim in pools and open water, and people who snorkel, and who want the time in the water to feel a bit more fun. It is not for diving. Finn is not a dive computer.

What you need

An Apple Watch with watchOS 26.5 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 26.5 or later.

Depth and water temperature need an Apple Watch Ultra, or a Series 10 or later. On other watches Finn still times your swim and brings animals.

Price

Finn is free to download. There are no ads and no account. Nothing changes which animals you meet or the odds.

What it does

A buddy on your wrist

One animal swims with you on the watch screen. Everyone starts with Shelly, a sea turtle. You can pick any animal you have met as your buddy.

Meet who lives in the water

After ten minutes in the water, someone new might turn up, at most once an hour. There are thirty animals. Where you swim decides who is around.

Fair odds, in the open

60% common, 30% uncommon, 8% rare, 2% legendary. They are the same for everyone. Staying longer or going deeper never improves them.

A logbook that remembers

Each swim is saved with its time, distance, pace and heart rate, and a strip of the water it happened in.

Works with Apple Health

Finn's swims are written to Health, so they show up in Fitness. Swims you logged with other apps can come in from Health.

Your aquarium on iPhone

Tap the water for bubbles. Make a wallpaper of your tank for your iPhone or your watch face.

Privacy

Finn has no account and no server. Your swims, animals and settings live on your Apple Watch and iPhone, and travel between them directly. Heart rate and other health numbers are saved to Apple Health and go nowhere else.

Read the privacy policy or the FAQ.

Safety

Finn is for fun. It gives no decompression, no-stop or ascent information, and it never asks you to go deeper or stay longer. Always swim and dive within your training, with proper instruments.
