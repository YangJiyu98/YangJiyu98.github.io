# Finn — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Finn is a swim and snorkel companion for Apple Watch, with an iPhone app that comes with
it. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version:
**Finn has no account and no server, and nothing you record leaves your own devices.**

## What Finn collects

- **Sensor readings from your watch, while a swim is in progress** — depth, water
  temperature, how long you were under, heart rate, distance, stroke count and energy
  burned.
- **Your location, once per swim, at the surface** — read before you go under, and used
  only to decide which animals live in the water you are in. It is never written to your
  logbook, never attached to anything you can share, and Finn records no route or track.
- **Swims you have logged in Apple Health** — when importing from Health is on, Finn
  reads swimming and underwater diving workouts, and their depth, water temperature,
  distance, stroke, energy and heart-rate samples, including sessions recorded by other
  apps, so your logbook is complete. You can switch this off in Finn's settings.

## What Finn does with it

- Stores your swims, your animal collection and your settings **on your Apple Watch and
  your iPhone**.
- **Sends swims from the watch to the iPhone directly**, over Apple's own connection
  between your paired devices. Nothing is routed through a server of ours, because there
  isn't one.
- **Writes each swim to Apple Health** on your device, so it sits in Fitness alongside
  your other activity. Finn writes workouts, underwater depth, water temperature,
  distance, stroke count and active energy. You can turn this off at any time.
- **Shows your last swim in Lock Screen and watch face widgets**, which read a small
  summary stored on the device itself.

## What Finn does not do

- **No servers and no account.** Your swims are never uploaded. There is nothing to sign
  in to.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting.** Health data is never
  used for advertising, never shared with any third party, and never sold.
- **No sharing between people.** There are no social features.
- **No route recording.** Finn takes one position fix at the surface and asks for no
  permission to record where you went.

## The diagnostic log

Finn can keep a diagnostic log on the device, which exists because the failures worth
fixing happen underwater, where there is no way to watch the app work. It records what
the app did — including heart-rate readings while a swim is running — to a file on your
watch, capped in size, oldest lines dropped first.

It is yours and it stays on your devices. Nothing in it is uploaded. You can read it, clear
it, or switch it off entirely in Finn's settings. If you ever send it to us to help with a
problem, that is you choosing to send a file, and nothing sends it on its own.

## Health data

Access is asked for once, when you first set Finn up, and covers everything Finn ever
reads or writes — so you are not asked again later. Health data is used solely to show you
your own swims inside Finn.

You can change or revoke it at any time in the **Health app → your profile picture →
Privacy → Apps → Finn**.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a swim in the logbook removes it from Finn. Animals you have already met stay in
your aquarium. Deleting the app removes Finn's data from that device. Swims already
written to Apple Health are managed in the Health app, and deleting Finn does not remove
them.

## Children

Finn is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from them.

## Safety

Finn is not a dive computer and must not be used as one. It is made for shallow water.
Always dive with proper instrumentation and training, and Finn will never ask you to go
deeper or stay longer.
