# Finn FAQ

Short answers about [Finn: Swim & Snorkel Buddy](/finn/), a free app for Apple Watch and iPhone.

## Price and what is free

**Is Finn free?** Yes. Finn is free to download from the App Store. It has no ads and no account.

**Can I pay to meet more animals or get better odds?** No. Who you meet depends on the water, not on you. The odds are 60% common, 30% uncommon, 8% rare and 2% legendary, the same for everyone. Staying longer or going deeper changes nothing.

## Privacy

**Does Finn need an account?** No. There is nothing to sign in to, and no server of ours.

**What stays on my devices?** Your swims, your animals and your settings live on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Swims go from the watch to the iPhone directly, over Apple's own connection between your paired devices.

**Where does my heart rate go?** Heart rate, energy, strokes, distance and laps are saved to Apple Health, where your other workouts are. Finn sends them nowhere else.

**Does Finn track where I swim?** No. Finn takes one position fix at the surface at the start of a swim to decide which animals live in the water you are in. It records no route.

**Is there analytics or advertising?** No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Full detail is in the [privacy policy](/finn/privacy).

## Which iPhones and Apple Watches

**What do I need?** An Apple Watch with watchOS 26.5 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 26.5 or later.

**Which watches show depth and water temperature?** Apple Watch Ultra, and Series 10 and later. On other watches, such as the SE 3, Finn still times your swim, counts it and brings animals. You start those swims yourself with Start swim.

**Does it work in a pool?** Yes. Set where you swim in the watch's Settings: Pool, with your pool's length, or Open water. A lap pool needs walls to turn at.

## Water safety and limits

**Is Finn a dive computer?** No. Finn gives no decompression, no-stop or ascent information. It is made for shallow water. Always swim and dive within your training, with proper instruments.

**How deep does it read?** The depth sensor reads to 6 metres. Below that Finn keeps counting time but cannot show depth.

**Will Finn warn me if something goes wrong?** No. Finn is not a safety device and does not replace a human buddy. See [snorkelling with a buddy](/finn/snorkelling-with-a-buddy-safety).

**What happens if I forget to end a swim?** On watches with a depth sensor, Finn ends and saves the swim itself after fifteen minutes out of the water. On other watches it runs until you end it.
